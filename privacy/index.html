Zombie Queen Privacy Policy (English)

Effective: [YYYY-MM-DD] · Developer: [developer/company] · Contact: [contact e-mail]

Summary — Zombie Queen (the "Game") does not collect your real name, phone number, precise location or contacts. Progress is saved on your device. We process only the minimum needed for anonymous statistics and, where used, rewarded ads. Core play never depends on any consent.

1. Data we process

  • Save data — stage progress, ratings, horde, currency (Brains), settings; a JSON file on your device; deleted with the app.
  • Anonymous installation ID — a random identifier generated by the analytics SDK; unrelated to your name, phone or e-mail; used for aggregate statistics (clear rates, stage duration).
  • Device info — device model, OS version, app version, language, and country at the SDK's aggregate level; used for crash and performance analysis.
  • Account link (optional) — if you choose "Continue with Apple / Google", only the platform-provided user identifier; we do not request your e-mail or name; deleted when you unlink or delete the account.
  • Advertising ID (optional) — processed by the ad SDK only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad (one revive, double reward).

We do not collect: real names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, precise location, contact lists, photos or microphone, event logs containing nicknames, or the full save file.

2. Third-party services

[Analytics SDK, e.g. Firebase Analytics], [Ads SDK, e.g. Google AdMob], Apple (Sign in with Apple), Google (Play Games / Sign-In). Each processes data under its own policy; the list is updated at release.

3. Children

The Game is intended for ages [12]+ and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. If we learn we have, we delete it promptly.

4. Your rights

You can delete on-device data at any time by deleting the app. To unlink an account, delete data or ask questions, contact us above; we respond within [30] days. Ad personalisation is controlled by your device settings (iOS tracking permission / Android ad personalisation opt-out).

5. Security and transfers

Save data stays on your device. Analytics and ad SDKs may process data on their providers' servers, including outside your country, under their policies.
